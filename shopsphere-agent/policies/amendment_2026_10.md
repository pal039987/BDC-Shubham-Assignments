# Refund Policy Amendment (October 2026)

## Effective date
This amendment applies to all refunds processed on or after 2026-10-01, regardless of when the order was placed. Where it conflicts with an earlier policy, this amendment wins.

## 1. Higher auto-refund limit for Premium customers
Premium customers may receive automatic refunds of up to Rs 8,000 per refund. The limit for Regular and Plus customers stays at Rs 5,000. Customers under fraud review must still be escalated for any refund, whatever their tier.

## 2. Shipping fee on wrong-item deliveries
When a refund is issued because the customer received the wrong item, the order's shipping fee is also refunded. Refunds for damaged or defective items still cover only the item price.
