# ShopSphere Shipping Policy
## Delivery times
Orders are usually delivered 3 to 7 days after they ship.

## Shipping fees
Regular customers pay a Rs 99 shipping fee per order. Plus and Premium customers get free shipping.

## Delayed shipments
If an order has been in "shipped" status for more than 7 days after its shipped date without being delivered, escalate the case to the Logistics team for a trace. Do not promise a refund for a delayed shipment; only Logistics can mark a shipment as lost.

## Lost shipments
When Logistics has marked an order as "lost", the customer is entitled to a full refund of the order amount, including the shipping fee, minus any refunds already issued on that order.

## Cancellations
Orders in "processing" status can be cancelled only by the Orders team. Escalate cancellation requests.

## Address changes
Delivery address changes are handled only by the Orders team. Escalate address change requests.
