# ShopSphere Refund Policy (2026 edition)

## Eligible reasons
A refund can be issued when an item arrives damaged, is defective, or is not the item the customer ordered (wrong item).

## Claim window
The customer must raise the claim within 15 calendar days of the delivery date. Claims raised after 15 days are not eligible for a refund.

## Refund amount
The refund equals the price of the affected item(s). Shipping fees are not refunded under this policy.

## What this policy does not cover
- Change-of-mind returns are handled by the Returns team, because they require a pickup.
- Lost shipments are covered by the Shipping Policy.
- ShopSphere does not pay monetary compensation for delivery delays.

## Scope and effective date
This edition applies to orders placed on or after 2026-07-01. Orders placed before that date continue to follow the 2025 edition.
