# ShopSphere Loyalty Program
## Tiers
- Regular: default tier for all customers.
- Plus: customers who spend Rs 25,000 or more in a calendar year.
- Premium: customers who spend Rs 75,000 or more in a calendar year.

## Benefits
- Plus and Premium: free shipping on every order.
- Premium: escalated tickets are handled by the priority support queue.
- All tiers: earn 1 loyalty point per Rs 100 spent; points can be redeemed at checkout.

## Notes
Tier does not change refund eligibility, claim windows, or refund limits.
